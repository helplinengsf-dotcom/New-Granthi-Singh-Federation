Who We Are

New Granthi Singh Federation is committed to serving humanity through community support, education, social welfare and compassionate action inspired by Sikh values.

ੴ

Our Mission

To encourage seva, unity and humanitarian service, and to support people and communities through responsible social initiatives.

🤝

Our Vision

To build a caring, united and inclusive society where people help one another with dignity, respect and compassion.

💛

Our Commitment

We aim to promote community participation, support those in need and work transparently toward meaningful social welfare.

Our Core Values

Our work is guided by service, honesty and respect for all.

Seva — Selfless Service
Helping others without discrimination.
Unity — Working Together
Bringing people together for positive change.
Humanity — Respect for Everyone
Treating every person with dignity and compassion.
Transparency — Responsible Work
Striving for honesty and accountability in our initiatives.

Our Areas of Service

We seek to encourage meaningful community support through activities such as education assistance, social welfare, volunteer participation, community events and help for people in need.

📚

Education Support

Encouraging learning and supporting educational opportunities.

🫶

Community Welfare

Promoting compassion, cooperation and support within communities.

🌍

Volunteer Service

Welcoming people who want to contribute their time and skills.
