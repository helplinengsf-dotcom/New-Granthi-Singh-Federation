1. Who is responsible

This policy describes how NGSF handles personal information through its website, login, AI Help Chat, and complaint services.

2. Information that may be collected

  • Account information: email, account identifier, and authentication records.
  • Complaint information: category, subject, description, reference number, status, timestamps, and details you choose to provide.
  • Role and assignment records: information needed to administer Coordinator and Admin permissions.
  • Technical information: limited security and diagnostic logs from hosting/database services.
  • AI messages: prompts and conversation text sent to the AI Help Chat.

Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, or sensitive personal information unless explicitly requested and necessary.

3. Purposes

Information may be used to create and secure accounts, provide support, register and route complaints, show status to authorised people, prevent abuse, maintain reliability, and meet legal obligations.

4. AI and third-party processing

If AI Chat is enabled, relevant message text and the context needed to answer may be sent to the configured AI provider. The provider processes that data under its own terms and NGSF's configuration. Do not describe AI messages as end-to-end encrypted unless a separately designed and tested system prevents the server and AI provider from reading them. AI responses may be wrong.

5. Who may access information

Access should be role-limited: users may access their own complaint records; coordinators may access complaints assigned to them; authorised administrators may access records needed to manage the service. Hosting, database, email, and AI providers may process data to provide their services. NGSF should not sell personal information. Explain any additional sharing required by law or consent before publication.

6. Security

NGSF aims to use HTTPS, managed authentication, least-privilege access, database row-level security, and protected server-side secrets. These controls must be configured and tested. No online transmission or storage method is perfectly secure. Privileged service-role keys and AI provider keys must never be placed in public HTML or a public repository.

7. Retention and deletion

Information should be kept only as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, resolve complaints, maintain appropriate records, or meet legal obligations. NGSF must decide and publish actual retention periods before launch. You may request access, correction, or deletion where applicable; some records may need to be retained for legitimate or legal reasons.

8. Your choices

For questions or requests to correct/delete information, contact helpline.ngsf@gmail.com. NGSF may verify your identity first. Never email passwords or one-time codes.

9. Children

The service is not designed to collect children's personal information without appropriate authorisation. Contact NGSF if you believe a child's information has been provided improperly.
